SpotCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

SpotCue ("the App") is a music practice app that lets you play along with songs from Spotify or Apple Music and record yourself. This policy explains what information the App collects, why, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

WhatWhyTied to your identity?
Email addressAccount sign-in (via Sign in with Apple, Google, or email) and support repliesYes
Account IDIdentifies your account across devices and app sessionsYes
Purchase history (lifetime unlock)Confirming you've purchased and unlocking the paid featuresYes
Feedback messages you submitOnly if you use the in-app feedback form; sent to us to respond to your messageYes

We do not collect audio recordings you make while practicing. Recordings are saved only in local storage on your own device and are never uploaded or transmitted anywhere.

We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Third-party services we use

ServicePurpose
Firebase (Google) — Authentication & FirestoreAccount sign-in and storing the account data listed above. See Google's Privacy Policy.
RevenueCatManages the one-time purchase and verifies your entitlement. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
Apple (Sign in with Apple, MusicKit, StoreKit)Account sign-in option, Apple Music playback, and processing your purchase. Apple, not SpotCue, handles your payment details.
SpotifyLets you search for and play songs from your own Spotify account (available to allow-listed beta testers). SpotCue does not store your Spotify credentials or listening history.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the email below — we'll delete it within 30 days except where we're required to keep records for legal reasons.

Children's privacy

SpotCue is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Contact us using the information below to exercise these rights.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.
